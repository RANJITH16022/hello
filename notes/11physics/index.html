Chapter 1: Units and Measurements

Dimensional Analysis & Error Propagation

Concept / Parameter Formula / Expression
Absolute Error Δa_i = |a_{mean} - a_i|
Mean Absolute Error Δa_{mean} = \frac{|\Delta a_1| + |\Delta a_2| + ... + |\Delta a_n|}{n}
Relative Error \frac{\Delta a_{mean}}{a_{mean}}
Percentage Error \delta a = \left(\frac{\Delta a_{mean}}{a_{mean}}\right) \times 100\%
Error in Sum/Difference (Z = A \pm B) ΔZ = \Delta A + \Delta B
Error in Product/Quotient (Z = \frac{AB}{C^2}) \frac{\Delta Z}{Z} = \frac{\Delta A}{A} + \frac{\Delta B}{B} + 2\frac{\Delta C}{C}

Chapter 2: Motion in a Straight Line

Kinematic Quantities & Uniform Acceleration Equations

Parameter Formula / Equation
Average Velocity v_{avg} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}
Instantaneous Velocity v = \frac{dx}{dt}
Instantaneous Acceleration a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2}
First Kinematic Equation v = u + at
Second Kinematic Equation s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2
Third Kinematic Equation v^2 = u^2 + 2as
Distance in n^{th} second S_n = u + \frac{a}{2}(2n - 1)

Chapter 3: Motion in a Plane

Vectors & Projectile Motion

Concept Formula / Expression
Resultant Vector Magnitude (Addition) R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + 2AB\cos\theta}
Direction of Resultant (α with \vec{A}) \tan\alpha = \frac{B\sin\theta}{A + B\cos\theta}
Scalar (Dot) Product \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta
Vector (Cross) Product |\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = AB\sin\theta \, \hat{n}
Projectile Trajectory Equation y = x\tan\theta - \frac{g}{2u^2\cos^2\theta}x^2
Time of Flight (T) T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}
Maximum Height (H) H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}
Horizontal Range (R) R = \frac{u^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}
Uniform Circular Motion (Centripetal Acc.) a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r

Chapter 4: Laws of Motion

Force, Momentum, Friction & Dynamics

Concept Formula / Expression
Linear Momentum \vec{p} = m\vec{v}
Newton's Second Law \vec{F}_{net} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = m\vec{a}
Impulse J = \vec{F}_{avg} \times \Delta t = \Delta \vec{p}
Static / Kinetic Friction Force f_s \le \mu_s N, \quad f_k = \mu_k N
Motion of Object on Level Circular Road v_{max} = \sqrt{\mu_s r g}
Vehicle on Banked Road (with friction) v = \sqrt{rg \left(\frac{\tan\theta + \mu_s}{1 - \mu_s\tan\theta}\right)}
Optimum Speed on Banked Road ( frictionless) v_0 = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta}
